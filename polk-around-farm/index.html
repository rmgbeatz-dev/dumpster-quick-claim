Good questions.

Don't see yours? Just text Monica. She usually answers faster than a contact form would.

What does “raw” honey actually mean?+
Our honey isn't heated or ultra-filtered. We strain out the wax bits and that's it, so pollen and natural enzymes stay in. Raw honey crystallizes over time, which is normal and a good sign. Warm the jar gently in water to loosen it.
When can I buy maple syrup?+
We make it in March, but we bottle enough to sell all year while supplies last. Syrup, maple cream, candy, sugar and spice rub are usually available. Bourbon maple is a smaller batch, so grab it when you see it.
Do I need an appointment for U-pick blueberries?+
Yes. Picking is by appointment only in July and August, and the best time is 8:30 am to noon. Call or text (810) 814-3699 to book.
Do you ship?+
Yes. Build your order above, choose “Ship it,” and Monica will follow up with shipping cost and payment details.
Can I order gift baskets for events or the holidays?+
Absolutely. Honey and maple gift baskets are some of our most popular items around the holidays, for teachers and for corporate gifts. Reach out early for large orders.
